AI-GCLM Readiness Assessment

A structured self-assessment of how far an organisation has integrated artificial intelligence into construction leadership judgment — not into its tooling. Twenty-four statements across the six pillars of the AI-GCLM framework produce a readiness profile and a prioritised set of next actions.

What this is. A practitioner instrument that operationalises the six-pillar framework published in AI-GCLM (IJETRM, 2025). It is a structured reflection aid, not a validated psychometric instrument — the underlying framework is conceptual and awaits empirical validation. Treat the output as a discussion starter for a leadership team, not a benchmark score. Your answers stay in your browser and are never transmitted anywhere.
